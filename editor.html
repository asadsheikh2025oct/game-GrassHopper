How to

Click or drag to paint. Right-click to erase. Every level needs one P (start) and one G (portal) or K (boss). Keep gaps ≤ 5 tiles and climbs ≤ 3 tiles per jump.

Ready Tip: the game opens in a new tab with your level. Come back here to keep editing.
